## Other loot council addons

Loot councils are a long-standing way of handing out loot in World of Warcraft, and addons such as RCLootCouncil are well known. ALC was written independently for Allemano's guild and is not a copy of any of them. The idea of an item strip along the council window, for example, is common to such addons.
